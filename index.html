        🚨 Emergency Safety Guide

        If you think money or details are already compromised — act fast, in this order.

        1. Money was sent or OTP shared

        1. Call your bank's number printed on your card or bank statement right now.
        2. Ask them to freeze / block your account and cards.
        3. Ask for a complaint/reference number and write it down.

        2. Report the fraud immediately

        1. Call Malaysia's National Scam Response Centre (NSRC) at 997 — reporting fast gives the best chance of stopping the payment.
        2. Note: official contact details can change — always verify the current NSRC number on official government websites.
        3. Save screenshots of the scam messages as evidence — do not delete the chat.

        3. If you shared your phone/email password

        1. Change that password immediately from a safe device.
        2. Sign out of all devices in your Google/Apple account settings.
        3. Turn on 2-step verification (2FA).

        4. Block and preserve

        1. Block the scammer's phone number and WhatsApp account.
        2. Do not delete chats or messages — they are evidence for the police and bank.
        3. Check your bank statements for unknown transactions.

        5. Tell someone

        • Scammers rely on silence. Inform a family member — it is not your fault.
        • Scamming is a crime; the police help victims of any age.
